JobAgent Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-17

JobAgent ("the app") is a Discord bot that matches your resume against a job posting and drafts a cold outreach email. This page explains what data it handles and why.

What we collect

  • Resume file and extracted text you upload in Discord, so job postings can be matched against it.
  • Job posting text or links you submit.
  • Gmail draft-creation access (the gmail.compose scope only) - if you connect your Google account, we use it solely to create draft emails in your own Gmail account. We cannot read, send, or delete your existing email, and we never see your Google password.

How it's used

Your resume text and the job posting are sent to Google's Gemini API to produce a match score and a drafted email. Nothing you submit is used to train any model, sold, or shared with third parties beyond what's needed to run this feature (Google's Gemini and Gmail APIs).

Storage and isolation

Each user's data (resume file, extracted resume text, Gmail token, job history) is stored in a record keyed to their Discord account. It is not visible to other users, including the app's operator, beyond what's needed for troubleshooting.

Deleting your data

To disconnect Gmail access at any time, revoke it directly from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions . To request deletion of stored resume/history data, contact the email below.
